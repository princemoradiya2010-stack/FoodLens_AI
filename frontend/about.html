About FoodLens

FoodLens AI

AI-powered Indian food recognition and nutrition estimation, designed to make meal tracking easier to understand.

How it works

From meal photo to food insights

01

Scan your meal

Upload a food image or capture one with your device camera.

02

AI detects food

The configured YOLO model returns food labels and individual detections.

03

Nutrition is estimated

Available nutrition mappings are used to calculate servings and meal totals.

04

Review before saving

Grouped foods can be reviewed and explicitly added to the meal journal.

05

Build your food history

Saved meal records are available in today's meals and meal history.

06

See available insights

Recommendations are provided by the existing FoodLens backend.

FoodLens estimates nutrition from its configured food mapping. Estimates depend on detection quality and available data and are not medical advice.

The project

Food tracking, made more understandable

FoodLens AI brings image-based food recognition together with nutrition estimates, meal journaling, and backend-generated recommendations. It does not require a user account and does not claim to replace professional nutrition or medical guidance.
